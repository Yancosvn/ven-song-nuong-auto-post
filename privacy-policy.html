Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Ven Sông Nướng Auto Post is an application that helps an authorized user transfer and publish video content from Google Drive to an authorized TikTok account.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the service may access:

  • Basic information associated with the authorized TikTok account.
  • Information required to publish content to the authorized TikTok account.
  • Video files selected or made available through the connected Google Drive workflow.

3. How We Use Information

Information and files accessed by the service are used only to:

  • Connect the authorized TikTok account.
  • Identify the authorized account where necessary.
  • Process video content selected for publishing.
  • Publish authorized video content to TikTok.
  • Operate and maintain the automation workflow.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information is shared with third-party services only when necessary to operate the requested integration, including TikTok and Google Drive.

5. Data Storage

The service is designed to use information only as necessary for the requested automation. Video files may remain in the user's Google Drive according to the user's own Google Drive settings and retention policies.

6. User Control

Users can disconnect or revoke access to connected services through the applicable Google or TikTok account settings.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect access to the service. Users should keep their Google, TikTok, and other account credentials confidential.

8. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or its integrations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Ven Sông Nướng through the business contact information provided on the official website.
